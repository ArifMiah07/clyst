# 📱 Clyst - Social Media and Personal Space Application

Clyst is a feature-rich social platform that blends the world of public interaction with private creativity. Designed to offer users both a vibrant social media experience and a personal sanctuary, Clyst empowers people to connect, share, and self-express—all in one app.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Social Media Core
- **Post Creation** – Users can create and share posts publicly with their network.
- **Friend System** – Add, manage, and interact with friends.
- **Group Creation** – Users can build or join groups for community-based interactions.
- **Search Functionality** – Powerful search to find users, groups, and content.

### 🪄 Personal Space (Private Mode)
Clyst goes beyond traditional social platforms by offering a personal creative space:
- **Private Journaling** – Write your thoughts like a digital diary or book.
- **Page & Note Creation** – Organize ideas or take quick notes.
- **Sketch & Draw** – Visual expression through drawing tools.
- **All data remains private** unless explicitly shared by the user.

## 💡 Inspiration

In a world flooded with noise, Clyst was born out of the need to balance **social connectivity** with **personal solitude**. It allows users to build relationships while also offering them a space for reflection, creativity, and mindfulness.
